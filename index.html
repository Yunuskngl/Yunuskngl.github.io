About

I like problems I can reason about, model and test. That comes from my background in mathematical engineering, and it’s how I approach engineering work: clear data, clear interfaces, code that holds up.

Most of my experience so far is in digital forensics. At TÜBİTAK BİLGEM I built data-extraction features for a forensics application, and my graduation thesis studied how iOS manages cryptographic keys. It led to dYKcrpytor, an open-source tool that decrypts the iOS Keychain.

Working with extracted data made me care about what happens next: turning raw records into something you can query, analyze and learn from. That is why I studied machine learning and why I’m now building with LLM APIs, alongside deepening ASP.NET Core and software architecture.
